# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Path of Exile 2 players who keep Regexile open beside the game, usually on a second monitor. They need to scan a mechanic or modifier combination, copy a reliable English-client regex, and return to the game with minimal interruption.

## Product Purpose

Regexile is a lightweight, source-controlled reference for useful Path of Exile 2 map and tablet regexes. Version 0.1 succeeds when players can find, understand, and copy a relevant pattern in seconds, while uncertain game strings are clearly separated from verified content.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, trustworthy. The interface should feel like focused developer tooling with a subtle Path of Exile companion identity: compact, calm in low-light play sessions, and confident without ornamental fantasy styling.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS dashboard, trading terminal, neon HUD, crypto interface, ornate medieval UI, glassmorphic product, live economy tracker, wiki replacement, or social platform. Avoid fake telemetry, fabricated prices, verification claims without supporting data, oversized pills, decorative glow, and marketing-page spacing.

## Design Principles

- Put the regex first: title, context, and status support the copy action rather than competing with it.
- Earn trust with explicit provenance: verified strings are distinct from concepts awaiting confirmation.
- Optimize for peripheral scanning: dense rows, predictable alignment, and restrained semantic accents.
- Keep every interaction immediate: keyboard search, direct copy feedback, and durable URLs.
- Preserve an expandable information architecture without building future tools prematurely.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA or better for text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks and headings, accessible names, 44px touch targets where needed, reduced motion, 200% zoom, and color-independent status communication. Regexile v0.1 is English-client and dark-mode only.
